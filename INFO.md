🚀 **Building a Life Insurance Pricing, Reserving & Financial Modelling Engine using Python**

As part of my actuarial learning journey, I am developing a Python-based Life Insurance Engine to understand how actuarial mathematics translates into practical insurance calculations and financial modelling.

The objective is to build a structured framework that can perform essential life insurance calculations and project policy cash flows.

**Key components I'm working on:**

🔹 Mortality and survival modelling  
🔹 Expected Present Value (EPV) calculations  
🔹 Life assurance and annuity valuations  
🔹 Premium calculations  
🔹 Policy cash-flow projections  
🔹 Initial and renewal expenses and commissions  
🔹 Death and maturity benefits  
🔹 Prospective policy reserves  
🔹 Surrender and paid-up values  
🔹 Bonus modelling  

**Technology Stack:**
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Jupyter Notebook
- Git and GitHub

I am developing this project incrementally, validating the calculations against actuarial principles and worked examples, with the goal of eventually extending it to more complex life insurance products.

This project is helping me connect my preparation for the Institute of Actuaries of India (IAI) examinations, particularly CM1, with practical Python programming and actuarial modelling.

I believe that combining actuarial knowledge with programming and data analytics is an important skill for the future of the insurance industry.

Feedback and suggestions from actuaries, actuarial students and data professionals are welcome!
